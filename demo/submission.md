# Blockplay SG — submission text

## Project name

Blockplay SG

## Description · what does it do?

Explore 3D Singapore neighbourhoods built with Astra from Google Maps visual references. Walk, drive, collect stamps, play FPS challenges, explore connected districts and learn with AI guides. Watch an AI pilot play, or take control. The workflow could bring more places into play.

## How did you use Astra?

As a team of two, we used Astra throughout development to turn Google Maps Street View references into recognisable, stylised Three.js worlds. Region agents worked in parallel, while shared browser capture and visual checks were coordinated in sequence. Astra helped implement walking, driving, collisions, collectibles, minimaps and AI companions, then diagnose issues and verify corrections.

Astra also helped build regional FPS challenges, connected-district expeditions and an AI player pilot that observes the game and acts through normal gameplay controls.

Our documented Raffles Place refinement shows this workflow in action: we captured and reviewed 25 new reference images, recorded visual observations, refined a facade, and compared matching-camera before/after renders. That refinement passed 200 automated tests and browser checks across all three regions.

Luna interprets companion requests, while GPT-Live-1 supports spoken interaction. Reference review, layout decisions, implementation and validation are already part of our agentic workflow, which can be repeated for more locations with human direction and review.
